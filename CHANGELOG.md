## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-16
### Added
- Personal blocklist with `/bb ignore`, `/bb warn`, `/bb remove` and `/bb list`. Each entry needs at least one reason and a comment.
- `/bb version` shows the installed version.
- English and German texts.
